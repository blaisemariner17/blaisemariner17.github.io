01 / Research

The questions behind the data.

My work spans molecular mechanisms, population-scale genomics, and the analysis systems that make ambitious biological studies possible. A recurring focus is understanding why individuals and species differ in aging and health.

01 / Published research

The molecular architecture of aging

With the Dog Aging Project, I investigate DNA methylation, transposable elements, biological age, and their relationship to lifespan. Our 2026 Science study identified distinct epigenetic patterns associated with sex and body size in dogs.

Read the Science paper ↗
02 / Ongoing research

From genetic variation to phenotype

I integrate genotype, epigenomic variation, and complex traits using GWAS, methylation-QTL mapping, Mendelian randomization, and colocalization—aiming to move beyond association toward more informative biological hypotheses.

Statistical genetics · Multi-omics
03 / Cross-species biology

Conserved mechanisms, different systems

My earlier work examined proteostasis, stress-response signaling, and lifespan regulation in experimental models. Current and collaborative projects extend that comparative approach to diverse genomic and molecular datasets.

02 / Expertise

Scientific depth, technical range.

I often work across the full research lifecycle—from designing the statistical approach and building the pipeline to interpreting results, developing figures, and shaping a manuscript.

Genomics & epigenomics

DNA methylation, epigenetic clocks, meQTLs, GWAS, allele-specific expression, genotype imputation, ancestry, comparative and multi-omic analysis.

Statistical inference

Mixed-effects and longitudinal models, high-dimensional testing, survival analysis, causal-inference methods, fine-mapping, colocalization, and sensitivity analyses.

Scalable scientific computing

R, Python, Bash, SLURM/HPC, reproducible pipelines, data integration, workflow validation, computational troubleshooting, and publication-ready visualization.
