Genetic Bioinformatician & Molecular Biologist

I design and deploy scalable genomic analysis workflows to uncover molecular mechanisms of aging, lifespan variation, and disease risk.

My work integrates epigenomics, GWAS, QTL mapping, and multi-omic modeling using production-grade HPC pipelines in R, Python, and Bash.

About

I specialize in high-throughput genomics and statistical genetics, translating large-scale -omic datasets into reproducible, interpretable biological insights.

  • Domains: Molecular Biology, Next-Gen Sequencing, single-cell RNA-sequencing, DNA methylation, epigenetic clocks, transposable elements, GWAS, meQTL, Mendelian Randomization
  • Tooling: R/Bioconductor (DESeq2/Limma/EdgeR, Seruat, BSseq, PQLSeq), Python, PLINK, GCTA, Nextflow, Samtools, bedtools
  • Infrastructure: SLURM-based HPC, containerized workflows, reproducible pipelines

Professional Appointments

Lead Genetic Bioinformatic Researcher
Dog Aging Institute & Dog Aging Project

I lead large-scale genomic and epigenomic analyses across 900+ dogs, investigating genetic architecture, methylation dynamics, and lifespan-associated molecular signatures. This work integrates GWAS, meQTL mapping, epigenetic clock modeling, and causal inference frameworks.

Collaborative Bioinformatics Research
Snyder-Mackler Lab , Arizona State University

I work closely with Noah Snyder-Mackler’s group on evolutionary genomics and aging biology, developing statistical pipelines and reproducible computational infrastructure for cross-species genomic analyses.
